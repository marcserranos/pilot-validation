Figures

What the review found

resultExpression confirms a germline deletion

Two assays from two workstreams agree on who lacks TRBV4-3, group by group. A usage cut-off of ≤ 0.1% matches the DNA rate. This is the most novel thing in the workstream and it is group-level only until the per-person join.

observationUnexplained locus effects

In TRBV4-3 non-users, TRBV7-2 (first gene after the insertion, not deleted) drops to 0.59×, and genes 5′ of the insertion rise 19–33%. Candidates: alleles in linkage with the non-insertion haplotype, or a positional effect on recombination. Not tested.

problemW5 gains are biased downward

LASSO puts one penalty on base and repertoire columns, so adding hundreds of columns shrinks age, sex and the ancestry PCs. That is why “+ all” falls below base (asthma 0.602 → 0.528). Adding B-cell chains, as asked, would make this worse until the base is held fixed.

correctionReport 05 enrichment counts

About half of the “significant” tests were depletions. The within-V odds ratios behind panel d's diamonds are degenerate. Report the CMH p/q values only.

observationImmune-mediated diseases lean the other way

For inverse Simpson, eight immune-mediated diseases have positive point estimates (fewer dominant clones) while cardiometabolic ones are negative. Only psoriasis is nominal (p 0.032, q 0.13). A direction to check, not a result.
